# Local engine recordings

These two loops were cut from WAV recordings supplied by the project owner.
On 2026-09-24, the owner confirmed that they have the rights to publish these
recordings on GitHub and in the online game. This confirmation concerns these
two engine loops, not unrelated music or third-party audio.

- `airliner-cruise.ogg`: 240–264 s of the supplied Boeing 737 MAX 9 takeoff
  recording, with a 1 s end-to-start crossfade. Used quietly for the 737-800
  and A380 during steady flight; the procedural layer still changes with RPM.
- `b2-cruise.ogg`: 33–40 s of the supplied B-2 flyby recording, with a 0.8 s
  end-to-start crossfade. Used for the B-2 in flight.

The original WAV recordings remain outside this repository, unchanged.
