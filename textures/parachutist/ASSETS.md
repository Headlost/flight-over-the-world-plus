# Parachutist texture sources

`face-albedo.png` and `nylon-albedo.png` were generated specifically for this game with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-09-15. The supplied character reference informed the original black/orange adult parachutist design. The current runtime materials reuse the neutral gray nylon albedo and tint the suit teal with blue accents; a subtle same-hue emissive contribution improves visibility without replacing or flattening the photographic cloth detail. No external portrait or textile photograph was copied. The original generated PNGs and their alpha channels are retained.

The quoted prompts below are retained as provenance records for the generated source textures and intentionally describe the palette requested at generation time.

Face prompt (verbatim):

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a production game character FACE ALBEDO TEXTURE, portrait ratio 3:4, full bleed face filling canvas. Primary request: high fidelity frontal orthographic skin texture of a realistic adult male parachutist, age 35–42, slightly tanned light olive/Caucasian skin, short dark stubble beard, closed neutral lips, strong but proportional nose and jaw, calm face, tiny pores and believable subtle facial creases. This is a flat front-facing texture map used on curved 3D face geometry, NOT a posed portrait photograph or full character. Framing: crop extremely tight, forehead at top edge, cheeks and sides cut off by left/right edges, chin at bottom edge. Canvas contains ONLY skin and facial features, absolutely no hair, clothing, accessories, background or neck. Head straight level, no tilt and no perspective. Feature layout normalized: symmetric eyes at x .28 and .72, y .33; nose tip x .50 y .52; closed mouth x .50 y .70; chin x .50 y .93. Broad forehead occupies y 0 through .25. Side edges are natural uniform cheek skin. The beard must be fine short individual stubble across lower cheeks, upper lip and chin, low contrast with skin, not a large solid beard patch. Lighting: diffuse uniform soft neutral white light without dramatic shadows, no directional cast shadows or specular glare, skin albedo suitable for physically lit game mesh. Details: subtle natural pores, slight lip texture, fine beard follicles. Avoid cartoon/anime/illustration, oversized eyes, eyeliner, artificial plastic skin, orange skin, smiles, white teeth, eye goggles or glasses, eyebrows too thick, hood, hairline, ears, outer background, vignettes, text or watermarks.

Nylon prompt (verbatim):

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless repeating PBR game clothing ALBEDO texture, square 1024x1024. Create an extremely realistic close texture of soft medium-gray technical nylon parachuting jumpsuit cloth. Flat square fabric fills all edges, subtle tiny woven threads, fine stitching-free wrinkles and crumples, irregular broad soft folds and small realistic diagonal compression creases, high fidelity photographic textile surface. A matte medium gray fabric, NOT black because shader will tint it to black or orange. Moderate soft tonal variation so wrinkles remain legible after dark tint, tiny thread detail and slight fabric grain. Diffuse neutral lighting without harsh directional highlights or shadows; no specular glare. The image must tile seamlessly in both axes with matching opposite edges, no borders. No seams, no labels, no straps, no pockets, no garment outline, no other objects, no text/watermark, no metallic/plastic or leather appearance, no regular checkerboard or geometric pattern, no large dramatic ridges. Intended as universal albedo+bump texture for a high-quality black/orange adult parachutist clothing model.

Both textures are loaded once per page and shared through the explicit `sharedModelTexture` lifetime marker. Each character owns its own material instances, including the material receiving role colors.
